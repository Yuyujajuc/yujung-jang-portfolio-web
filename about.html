About Resume

Hello! I am a graphic designer focusing on motion graphics and kinetic typography, illustrative lettering, data visualization and other visual communication. I am studying graphic design at Rhode Island School of Design, currently in my senior year.

Email is the best way to contact me:
yjang03@risd.edu

Growing up, I have been sensitive to what I see. I would trace a calligraphy logo on a pizza box or arrange the placement of stickers and sticky notes on my journal. When I read, I would flip the pages back and forth to see how the body of text creates a rhythmic outline. In the second year of my graphic design practice in RISD I learned that graphic design is nothing abstract, it was the visual decisions I have been making. I am still exploring graphic design as a whole because there are a lot of things that excite me!

When I don't create digital works, you can find me playing the guitar in my room or swimming. If you don't see me in either place, I am probably in front of a full-body mirror, picking the day's outfit that expresses my mood with 105% accuracy.
