Hello! I am a designer, illustrator, animator, and AI user studying graphic design at Rhode Island School of Design, currently in my senior year. Email is the best way to contact me.

Growing up, I have been sensitive to what I see. I would trace calligraphic logos on a pizza box or arrange the placement of stickers and sticky notes, and, that wasn’t enough, so I followed the rhythmic outline formed by the ragged edges of text in books. In the second year of my graphic design practice in RISD I learned that graphic design doesn’t have to be grandiose, it’s all those things that excite me already. How exciting is that?

When I don't create digital works, you can find me playing the guitar in my room or swimming. If you don't see me in either place, I am probably in front of a full-body mirror, picking the day's outfit that expresses my mood with 105% accuracy.
